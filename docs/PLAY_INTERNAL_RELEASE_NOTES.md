# Internal Testing Release Notes

## Sholatku 0.1.0 (versionCode 1)

Initial Internal Testing candidate of Sholatku.

Includes:

- Prayer schedules with location and calculation preferences.
- Next-prayer countdown and best-effort local prayer reminders.
- Quran reading, search, favorites, bookmarks, offline text, and optional audio playback.
- Monthly prayer schedules and Qibla direction.
- Ramadan information and settings.
- Local Privacy Policy route and settings access.

Known limitations for testers:

- The Internal Testing candidate intentionally uses the documented staging BFF; it is not the production backend.
- Qibla behavior depends on device sensors and calibration.
- Inexact Android reminders may be delayed by Doze, force-stop, or OEM power management.
- Full physical/cloud real-device coverage is still being completed.
- This candidate has not been uploaded or rolled out to Google Play by automation.
