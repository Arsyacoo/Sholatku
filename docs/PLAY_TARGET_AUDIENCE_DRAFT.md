## Provisional product classification

- Sholatku is a general prayer-time and Quran companion.
- It is not designed specifically for children.
- The app has no account system, advertising SDK, child-directed social features, or user-generated content.
- The final age bands must be selected by the developer in Play Console based on the intended audience, not inferred only
  from the code.
