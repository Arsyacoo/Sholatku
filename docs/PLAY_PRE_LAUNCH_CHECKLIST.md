# Play Pre-Launch Checklist

Status: preparation only. No Play Console pre-launch report exists until an AAB is uploaded manually.

When Google Play produces a report for the Internal Testing release, review:

- Crashes and ANRs across device/API configurations.
- Accessibility findings, TalkBack labels, touch targets, font scaling, and contrast.
- Network security and clear-text traffic warnings.
- Device compatibility and orientation behavior.
- Notification permission and reminder delivery observations.
- Screenshots, app label, icon, privacy policy, and store metadata consistency.

Do not describe the report as passed before the actual Play Console report is available.
